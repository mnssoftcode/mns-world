- **PHASE 11 — Visa / Sponsorship**
- This becomes important once you have a realistic job pathway.
- Australia currently has employer-sponsored **Skills in Demand (subclass 482)** pathways; Home Affairs says applicants need an approved sponsor, relevant skills, and the applicable English requirement. The Core Skills stream also requires at least one year of relevant work experience in the nominated occupation or a related field. ([Immigration and citizenship Website](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/Visa-subsite/Pages/work/skills-in-demand-482-landing.aspx))
- So your eventual checklist should include:
- Identify likely nominated occupation
- Check occupation list
- Determine relevant occupation mapping
- Check qualification requirements
- Check experience evidence
- Check English requirement
- Identify sponsoring employers
- Understand employer nomination process
- Check visa costs
- Prepare police documents
- Prepare health requirements
- Prepare visa application
- Visa grant
- There are also permanent employer-sponsored options such as subclass 186 and regional employer-sponsored subclass 494; eligibility differs by stream. ([Immigration and citizenship Website](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/employer-subsite/Pages/compare-sponsored-skilled-visa-options.aspx))
- **Don't obsess over this phase yet.** The occupation/visa details should be verified against the rules in force when you're actually applying.
